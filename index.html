Practice for Texas grades 6 to 8, on a laptop that stays on task.

A parent locks the family Windows laptop to Studywork. The student answers real TEKS questions in math, reading, science and social studies, and the games open once they have answered one question correctly in each subject that day.

A missed question shows how a similar problem is solved, so the student learns the method and then tries again. Each student has their own progress, and the parent sees it without a login.

The student's question

A shirt costs $40 and is discounted 25%. What is the sale price in dollars?

They answer 35. That is wrong, so Studywork does not show 30. It shows a different problem instead.

A worked example with different numbers

A $60 jacket is discounted 25%. What is the sale price in dollars?

  1. Find the discount first: 25% of $60 is 0.25 × 60 = $15.
  2. Subtract the discount from the original price: 60 − 15 = 45, so the sale price is $45.

Method: find the discount, then subtract it.

What the study lock stops, and what it does not

While the lock is on

  • Studywork fills the screen and stays in front of other windows.
  • The Windows key, Win+Tab, Alt+Tab, Alt+Esc and Ctrl+Esc do nothing (version 3.23 and newer).
  • If a touchpad swipe, Task View or a minimise moves the window away, it comes back.
  • Closing the window, reloading and developer tools are blocked.
  • Only the parent PIN ends the lock. Five wrong guesses start a wait that doubles each round, up to 15 minutes.

What it cannot do

  • Ctrl+Alt+Del still opens the Windows security screen. No app can block it.
  • It does not stop a student from shutting the laptop down or signing out.
  • It does not lock other Windows accounts on the same laptop.
  • It is not a school-grade lockdown. For one, Windows Assigned Access can limit a laptop to a single app.

Pricing

$5 a month per household

That is the plan for Studywork Family: every course, all the games and every student in the household. Subscriptions are not switched on in the current app, so nothing is charged and everything is open. If that changes, the app will tell you before it asks for anything.

Where the data lives

Student profiles, progress and study sessions are saved on the laptop, in a local database, and practice works with no internet. A parent can also sign in to an account to back up progress and join study groups. The account is optional, and without it a forgotten PIN cannot be reset by email.
